Common questions

  • Does Flaggle work offline? Yes — all flags and country data are bundled, so you can play with no internet. (Ads are the only part that uses the network.)
  • How do I play with friends? Tap Party Mode on the home screen to pass one device between 2–6 players or teams.
  • How is my score saved? Scores, streaks and settings are stored on your device. There's no account and no sign-up.
  • How do I change the language or theme? Open Settings (the gear icon) to switch themes. Flaggle supports English and Arabic.
  • The ads — can I report an inappropriate one? Yes, email us the details and we'll block it.
