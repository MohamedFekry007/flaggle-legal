Privacy Policy for Flaggle

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pairwise ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile application Flaggle ("the App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The game itself is offline — all flags, country data, and your scores, streaks and settings stay on your device. We do not require an account and never ask for your name, email or password.
  • To keep the App free, we use ads and basic analytics/crash reporting from third-party services (Google) that collect limited technical data as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Information collected

We do not directly collect personally identifying information. The third-party services we use collect the following to function:

1. Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

  • App usage and interactions (screens viewed, games played, features used)
  • Device and software information (device model, OS, language, country/region)
  • A randomly generated app-instance identifier

2. Crash reporting — Google Firebase Crashlytics

  • Crash logs, diagnostic data and device state at the time of a crash

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

  • A device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device information and general (coarse) location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data

Where required (e.g. the EEA/UK) we show a consent form; on iOS we request App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are non-personalized.

4. Purchases — Apple App Store / Google Play

If you buy "Flaggle Premium", the transaction is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment details. The App only stores a local flag indicating that ads are removed.

How information is used

  • To operate, maintain and improve the App
  • To show and measure advertising that keeps the App free
  • To diagnose crashes and fix problems

We do not sell personal data and do not build advertising profiles ourselves — any ad personalization is performed by Google subject to your consent.

Sharing

Data is processed by our service providers Google (Firebase, AdMob) and the app stores (Apple, Google Play) under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Data retention

On-device data (scores, settings) remains until you delete the App. Analytics and crash data are retained per Google's default retention settings. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: reset/limit your advertising ID in iOS or Android settings; decline tracking in the consent / ATT prompt for non-personalized ads.
  • Delete data: uninstalling the App removes all on-device data. To request deletion of analytics/crash data associated with your device, contact us.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK) / CCPA (California): you may request access to or deletion of your personal data; California residents may opt out of "sale/share" (we do not sell). Contact us.

Children's privacy

Flaggle is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change and the current version will always be at this page.
